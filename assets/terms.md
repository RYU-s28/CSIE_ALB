# CSIE Attendance Bot
## Terms of Use and Service Policy
**使用條款與服務政策**

**Effective Date:** October 12, 2026  
**Service:** CSIE Attendance LINE Bot  
**Platform:** LINE Official Account

### 1. General Provisions

The CSIE Attendance Bot (hereinafter referred to as the "Service") is an automated student attendance and work-related information management system designed to assist participating students in recording, monitoring, and reviewing their attendance and leave information.

By accessing, registering for, or using the Service, users acknowledge that they have read, understood, and agreed to comply with these Terms of Use.

The Service is intended to facilitate administrative coordination and personal record management. It does not replace official attendance procedures, employer regulations, or institutional policies.

### 2. Eligibility and User Registration

2.1. The Service is intended exclusively for students and other individuals authorized by the designated system administrators.

2.2. Users may be required to register their LINE account and provide information necessary for identification, including their name, student identification number, and assigned work group.

2.3. Each registered account shall be associated with an individual user. Users are responsible for ensuring the accuracy of the information submitted during registration.

2.4. Unauthorized access, impersonation, or deliberate submission of false information is strictly prohibited.

### 3. Attendance and Leave Management

3.1. The Service may automatically identify and process attendance-related messages submitted through designated LINE group conversations or private interactions.

3.2. Attendance information may be classified into the following categories:

- Present (出席)
- Sick Leave (病假)
- Personal Leave (事假)
- Menstrual Leave (經痛／生理假)
- Special Leave (特休)
- Half-Day Leave (半天)

3.3. Automated classifications are generated through predefined processing rules and, where applicable, artificial intelligence. These classifications may be subject to errors or misinterpretation.

3.4. Users are responsible for verifying the accuracy of their attendance and leave records.

3.5. The recording of a leave request in the Service does not constitute official approval by the employer, school, or other authorized personnel.

3.6. Any discrepancy must be reported through the designated correction or support procedure.

### 4. Attendance Statistics and Reporting

4.1. Attendance percentages and summaries are generated using the available attendance records and configured working calendar.

4.2. Current-month attendance rates shall be calculated based on elapsed scheduled workdays rather than the entire calendar month's scheduled working days.

4.3. Future workdays shall not be treated as absences solely because they have not yet occurred.

4.4. Statistics may change following record corrections, changes to work schedules, or administrative adjustments.

4.5. Reports generated by the Service are provided for informational and administrative reference unless expressly recognized as official records by the relevant institution or employer.

### 5. Salary Inquiry and Financial Estimates

5.1. The Salary Inquiry feature provides users with access to available payroll information and estimated earnings calculations.

5.2. Estimated salaries may be calculated using recorded attendance, configured working hours, hourly wage rates, overtime, allowances, and applicable deductions.

5.3. Calculations involving labor insurance, national health insurance, income tax, and other deductions are estimates unless derived from verified payroll records.

5.4. The Service does not guarantee that estimated earnings correspond exactly to the salary ultimately paid by an employer.

5.5. Official payslips, employer payroll records, and legally applicable regulations shall prevail in the event of any discrepancy.

5.6. The Service does not independently process salaries, transfer funds, or authorize payroll payments.

### 6. Privacy and Data Protection

6.1. The Service may process information reasonably necessary for its operation, including:

- LINE user identifiers and registered profile information
- Student identification and work-group information
- Attendance dates, status, and leave classifications
- Work schedules and related administrative records
- Payroll settings and salary information, where enabled
- Support requests and relevant system activity records

6.2. Collected information shall be used for attendance management, authorized reporting, service operation, troubleshooting, and related administrative purposes.

6.3. Individual attendance histories, payroll information, and other personal records shall be accessible only to the respective user and personnel with legitimate authorization.

6.4. Information submitted in designated group conversations may be visible to other group participants in accordance with LINE's platform functionality.

6.5. The Service may utilize third-party infrastructure and processing services, including LINE, cloud hosting providers, Google Sheets, and artificial intelligence service providers.

6.6. Reasonable technical and administrative safeguards shall be implemented to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

6.7. Users may submit requests regarding their personal records through the designated administrative or support channels, subject to applicable retention requirements.

### 7. Artificial Intelligence and Automated Processing

7.1. Certain functions may utilize artificial intelligence to interpret messages, recognize attendance-related intentions, and assign preliminary leave classifications.

7.2. AI-generated results are not guaranteed to be complete, accurate, or free from error.

7.3. Automated determinations may be reviewed, corrected, or overridden by authorized administrators.

7.4. Users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive personal details, medical information, or confidential documents through ordinary chat messages.

7.5. The Service shall not be considered an authoritative source for medical, legal, financial, or employment-related decisions.

### 8. Acceptable Use

Users agree not to:

- Submit knowingly false or misleading attendance information.
- Impersonate another student or authorized administrator.
- Attempt to access another user's confidential records.
- Exploit system vulnerabilities or circumvent access restrictions.
- Interfere with the Service's operation.
- Misuse automated functions, administrative commands, or support systems.

Violations may result in restricted access, temporary suspension, or referral to the appropriate administrator.

### 9. Administrative Authority

9.1. Authorized administrators may review attendance records, address discrepancies, maintain working calendars, and perform necessary system operations.

9.2. Administrative modifications should be recorded and traceable to maintain transparency and accountability.

9.3. Administrative access shall be limited according to assigned responsibilities.

9.4. Service administrators do not automatically possess the authority to approve official employment leave, determine payroll entitlements, or modify institutional records outside the Service.

### 10. Service Availability and Limitations

10.1. The Service is provided on an "as available" basis and may experience interruptions due to maintenance, network issues, third-party service failures, or technical limitations.

10.2. Reasonable efforts shall be made to maintain service reliability and data accuracy.

10.3. The Service shall not be regarded as the sole method of notifying an employer or institution of an absence.

10.4. Users remain responsible for complying with all official reporting procedures and deadlines.

### 11. Amendments to the Terms

The Service administrators may revise these Terms to reflect changes in functionality, operational requirements, institutional procedures, or applicable regulations.

Material amendments should be communicated through an appropriate official channel.

Continued use of the Service following notification of revised Terms constitutes acceptance to the extent permitted by applicable law.

### 12. Governing Rules

These Terms shall be interpreted consistently with applicable laws and regulations of Taiwan, including relevant personal data protection requirements.

Where these Terms conflict with mandatory legal requirements, the applicable law shall prevail.

### 13. Contact and Support

For questions, attendance corrections, account-related concerns, or inquiries regarding personal information, users may contact the authorized administrators through the Service's Help Center (服務中心) or its designated official support channel.
